How the learning loop works

  1. A death is pronounced: the patient is archived and every open order, reminder and booking stops.
  2. A doctor writes the WHO certificate; a draft study case is prepared automatically.
  3. Where the family agrees (or the law requires), pathology performs an autopsy and compares its findings with the team’s diagnosis.
  4. The M&M meeting decides whether the death was preventable and what to change.
  5. A reviewer checks the anonymised case and publishes it to the clinical library — unless the family declined.
