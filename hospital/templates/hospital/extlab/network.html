How it works

  1. You are notified when the hospital sends a patient.
  2. The patient arrives: type their phone number. Only requests addressed to {{ fac.name }} can be found.
  3. Record the sample, then enter the results or attach your report.
  4. Results go straight into the patient's hospital record.

Every search is recorded. A request stays open for you for 8 hours after the phone number is entered.
